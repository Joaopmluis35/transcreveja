Help & Support

Ouviescrevi turns audio, video and text into transcriptions and useful AI tools — right in your browser. Here are answers to the most common questions:

1. What is Ouviescrevi?

A platform built in Portugal that uses AI to transcribe files, generate summaries, translate, proofread, create study questions, subtitle videos and more. You can try it without an account; a free account gives you higher limits and saved history.

2. Do I need an account?

Not to get started. Most tools work immediately. A free account increases your daily transcription limit and saves your history. The Pro plan unlocks extra quotas and DOCX export.

3. Which file formats are supported?

Audio and video: MP3, MP4, WAV, M4A, MOV and other common formats. You can also paste text directly or use a URL on tools like summary and questions.

4. What features are available?

  • 🎙️ Transcription — convert audio and video to editable text
  • 📌 AI summary — formal, simple, bullet points or meeting minutes
  • ❓ Study questions — multiple choice with answer key, export and classroom test template
  • 🌍 Translation — into multiple languages
  • ✍️ Proofreading — spelling and grammar
  • 📧 Email — draft messages from text or transcripts
  • 🎬 Subtitles — SRT files and burned-in captions
  • 🗂️ Classification — detect content type (class, meeting, podcast…)
  • 📄 Converter — between document and audio formats

5. Is transcription 100% accurate?

Quality depends on audio clarity and background noise. We use OpenAI's Whisper model, which performs very well. We recommend reviewing the text before sharing or publishing.

6. Are my files stored?

Audio and video files are processed and deleted after transcription. We keep minimal metadata (file name, date) for statistics and your account history. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

7. How do I use the AI tools?

On the home page, upload a file or paste text. After transcription, choose an action — summarize, translate, proofread, classify or generate an email. On dedicated pages (like Questions or Summary) you can work directly with text or a URL.

8. How do study questions work?

Paste a lesson, article or chapter, choose how many questions you want, and the AI generates A–D options with answers and explanations. You can copy, export to PDF, share on WhatsApp or create a formatted classroom test sheet to print.
