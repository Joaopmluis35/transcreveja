Datenschutzerklärung

This policy describes how Ouviescrevi (ouviescrevi.pt) processes personal data. We recommend legal review before large-scale commercial use.

1. Data controller

Controller: Ouviescrevi — ouviescrevi@gmail.com

2. Data we process

  • Usage data (pages visited, timestamps, anonymized daily visitor identifier; IP may appear in server logs)
  • Files you upload (audio, video, documents)
  • AI-generated outputs (transcripts, summaries, translations)
  • Filename and date for internal statistics
  • Support emails you send voluntarily
  • Browser storage (API session token, language preference — see Cookie-Richtlinie)

We do not require account registration for core features.

3. Purposes & legal bases (GDPR)

  • Provide transcription and AI features — contract / pre-contractual steps
  • Security and abuse prevention — legitimate interest
  • Aggregated usage statistics — legitimate interest
  • Legal compliance — legal obligation

4. Uploaded files

Files are sent to our API, processed temporarily, and sent to OpenAI when you request transcription or AI features. Temporary server files are deleted after processing. We store only filename and timestamp for statistics — not audio content.

You are responsible for having rights to upload content and for obtaining consent from recorded individuals where required.

5. Processors & international transfers

  • OpenAI (USA) — Whisper & GPT
  • Render — API hosting
  • Cloudflare — static site & DNS

Transfers outside the EEA rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

6. Retention

  • Uploaded files: minutes (deleted after processing)
  • Statistics (filename + date): until deletion requested
  • Server logs: per hosting provider retention

7. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and complaint to your supervisory authority.

Portugal: CNPD — www.cnpd.pt

Contact: ouviescrevi@gmail.com (response within 30 days).
